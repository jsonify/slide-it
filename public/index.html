      How to play

      1. Everyone gets the same scrambled tray of 24 coloured tiles and one gap.
      2. When the countdown ends, a target pattern appears.
      3. Tap any tile in the same row or column as the gap to slide it — tiles in between move too. You can also swipe.
      4. First to make the middle 3×3 of their tray match the target wins the round. The outer ring doesn't matter.
      5. Win enough rounds to take the match. Watch your friends' trays update live!
